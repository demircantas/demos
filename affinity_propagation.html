How it works

Similarity is s(i,k) = −‖xᵢ − xₖ‖². The diagonal s(k,k) is the preference: how willing each point is to be an exemplar.

r(i,k) ← s(i,k) − maxk′≠k[a(i,k′) + s(i,k′)]
a(i,k) ← min(0, r(k,k) + Σi′∉{i,k} max(0, r(i′,k)))

Responsibility r(i,k): point i tells k how much better k suits it than its best alternative. Availability a(i,k): k tells i how much support it has from other points. Points where r(k,k)+a(k,k) > 0 become exemplars. Damping λ blends old and new messages to stop oscillation.

Higher preference gives more exemplars. Like k-means, it favors compact, roundish clusters (try Two moons), and it stores N² messages.

How it works

Similarity is s(i,k) = −‖xᵢ − xₖ‖². The diagonal s(k,k) is the preference: how willing each point is to be an exemplar.

r(i,k) ← s(i,k) − maxk′≠k[a(i,k′) + s(i,k′)]
a(i,k) ← min(0, r(k,k) + Σi′∉{i,k} max(0, r(i′,k)))

Responsibility r(i,k): point i tells k how much better k suits it than its best alternative. Availability a(i,k): k tells i how much support it has from other points. Points where r(k,k)+a(k,k) > 0 become exemplars. Damping λ blends old and new messages to stop oscillation.

Higher preference gives more exemplars. Like k-means, it favors compact, roundish clusters (try Two moons), and it stores N² messages.

Inside the matrices

Everything comes from one N × N similarity matrix. Distance is used once, to fill S. Responsibility and availability are then sums, differences and maxima of S and of each other, so every value below is in units of squared distance.

Read it as an election

Every point is both a voter and a candidate. Rounds of votes and replies repeat until the result stops changing.

Preference s(k,k) = p
How highly each point rates itself as a candidate. Think of −p as the filing fee for running: a high preference (near 0) makes running cheap, so many candidates survive; a very negative one leaves room for only a few.
Responsibility, a vote r(i,k)
Voter i tells candidate k how far ahead it is of i's best alternative. Computed across row i: candidates compete for each voter.
Availability, a reply a(i,k)
Candidate k tells voter i how much backing it has: its self-vote plus everyone else's positive votes. Computed down column k: voters pool their support. Capped at 0, so a reply can warn a voter off but never bribe one.
The count r(k,k) + a(k,k) > 0
A candidate whose self-vote plus backing is positive is elected as an exemplar. Each point then joins the elected candidate it rates highest.

S, similarity from the points

s(i,k) = −‖xᵢ − xₖ‖² s(k,k) = p

Each point's rating of every candidate, fixed for the whole run. The diagonal is the preference p, set here from the median off-diagonal similarity.

R, responsibility from S and A

r(i,k) ← s(i,k) − maxk′≠k[a(i,k′) + s(i,k′)]

A vote from i to k: how much better k suits i than i's best alternative. With A = 0 it is just the gap to i's runner-up.

A, availability from R

a(i,k) ← min(0, r(k,k) + Σi′∉{i,k} max(0, r(i′,k))) a(k,k) ← Σi′≠k max(0, r(i′,k))

A reply from k to i: how much backing k has. Only positive votes count.

Decision from R + A

exemplar k ⇔ r(k,k) + a(k,k) > 0 m ← λ·mold + (1−λ)·mnew

The diagonal of R + A is the evidence shown as halos. Every message is damped by λ before it is stored.
